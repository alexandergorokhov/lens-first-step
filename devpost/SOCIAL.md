## LinkedIn

I built an indoor navigation Lens for Snap Specs, without ever wearing Specs.

There's no GPS inside an airport or a mall. With glasses, the way can sit on the floor in front of you while your hands stay on the bags, the stroller or the wheelchair. You say where you want to go in your own words ("coffee and a bathroom before my flight"), and a companion walks you there, step-free if you need it.

What I learned:
- You can build for glasses without the glasses. The Lens Studio preview plus 61 in-Lens tests carried the whole trip.
- Write the test first, even in AR. It caught bugs I'd never have seen by eye.
- Step-free is a rule, not a filter. Even when the AI picks the place, the router checks there's a way in without stairs.
- Additive displays can't show black. Design light, keep the screen almost empty while walking.
- Pairing with an AI agent (Claude, through Lens Studio's MCP) moved my job from typing code to deciding what the product is and checking every claim.
